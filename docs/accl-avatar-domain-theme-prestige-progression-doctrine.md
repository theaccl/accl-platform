# ACCL Avatar, Domain Theme, Trainer Theme, and Prestige-Progression Doctrine

This document locks product doctrine for future phased implementation. It is **docs-only** until explicitly approved for engineering work.

---

## 1. Core Identity Principle

ACCL player themes are not generic profile-image filters.

They are **persistent identity domains**.

A player begins by choosing one foundational ACCL color identity. That identity may later evolve through earned progression, selected theme packages, and prestige-material transformations.

The system should create the feeling that other users are stepping into the player’s own ACCL-aligned domain.

**Examples:**

- a red carnival-jester court
- a black gothic chess theater
- a white ceremonial chess hall
- a blue tactical command citadel

The theme should affect more than the player image. It may eventually influence profile presentation, domain scenes, Trainer styling, banners, cards, and selected showcase surfaces.

---

## 2. Starting Base Theme Selection

When a player begins their ACCL identity setup, they choose one locked base color theme from four foundational options:

- **Red**
- **Blue**
- **Black**
- **White**

This is not a freely swappable color picker.

The starting choice becomes part of the player’s long-term visual identity.

### Base-theme meaning

| Base | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Red** | Attack, pressure, chaos, fire, momentum, trickster energy, tactical aggression. |
| **Blue** | Strategy, calculation, order, discipline, command, royal control, positional planning. |
| **Black** | Shadow, intimidation, dominance, menace, psychological pressure, endgame energy. |
| **White** | Honor, prestige, ceremony, nobility, clarity, ascension, championship presence. |

---

## 3. Locked Base-Theme Rule

The initial base theme is persistent and should not be casually changed.

A player should not be able to freely rotate between red, blue, black, and white whenever desired.

At selected progression milestones, ACCL may grant a new theme-choice opportunity.

When granted, the player may:

- keep the existing base theme
- choose another available base theme
- apply the newly selected base theme to the current ACCL-generated identity image
- use the newly selected base theme on a future personal upload transformation

The exact milestone schedule remains an implementation decision.

---

## 4. Historical-Peak Authority

Theme-choice rewards and prestige unlocks are governed by the player’s **highest confirmed achievement**, not by temporary rating border movement.

A reward should trigger only when the player surpasses their prior confirmed peak milestone.

The following must **not** produce duplicate unlocks:

- upgrade
- downgrade
- return to the same previously reached level
- repeat downgrade
- repeat re-upgrade
- rating fluctuation around the same border

**Core rule:** The player must surpass their prior highest confirmed achievement before earning a new progression reward.

The system must distinguish:

- current competitive status
- highest confirmed status ever achieved
- permanent cosmetic unlock history

## 10. Trainer Theme Doctrine

Trainer should support the same player identity system, but Trainer must use a **cleaner and more functional** interpretation.

Trainer must prioritize:

- board readability
- text readability
- move-list clarity
- puzzle clarity
- analysis clarity
- stable controls
- low visual distraction

Trainer should not become a full cinematic environment behind every control.

### Trainer dominant-layer rule

Trainer should use the player’s current **dominant color or prestige material** as the main visual foundation.

**Examples:**

- solid Blue Trainer shell
- solid Black Trainer shell
- Gold-dominant Trainer shell with Blue accents
- Platinum-dominant Trainer shell with Gold accents

### Trainer accent rule

Style packages and earned progression should appear mainly as **supporting accents**:

- trim
- framing
- panel borders
- selected-state highlights
- icons
- crest details
- motifs
- subtle patterns
- lighting
- decorative corners
- completion effects
- achievement-card treatment
- restrained ambient environment details

Trainer should feel owned and personalized without sacrificing usability.

## 12. Prestige Progression Principle

Prestige progression should not appear only at full promotion boundaries.

Each major level contains an **internal visual upgrade state**.

The player should visibly advance within a level before officially reaching the next level.

**Core visual sequence:**

**current dominant material → next material appears as accents → next material becomes dominant after formal promotion**

---

## 13. Prestige Accent Phase

The level immediately before a new prestige material should introduce that upcoming material as accents.

**Example:** A Blue-domain player approaching Gold receives:

**Blue with Gold Accents**

Blue remains dominant.

Gold appears selectively in:

- trim
- crest edges
- banners
- borders
- lighting
- framing
- armor details
- environment details
- Trainer card highlights
- decorative effects

The player is not Gold yet.

The visual upgrade communicates advancement within the current level.

---

## 14. Prestige Inversion Rule

When the player officially reaches the next prestige-material level, the hierarchy flips.

**Example:**

**Blue with Gold Accents** becomes **Gold with Blue Accents**

Gold becomes dominant.

Blue remains as identity lineage.

This preserves the player’s origin while making the promotion visually unmistakable.

The original base color should never disappear completely. It should remain traceable through restrained identity details.

---

## 15. Full Example Ladder

For a player whose original base color is **Blue**:

1. **Blue**
2. **Blue with Gold Accents**
3. **Gold with Blue Accents**
4. **Gold with Platinum Accents**
5. **Platinum with Gold Accents**
6. **Platinum with Diamond Accents**
7. **Platinum-Diamond with lineage accents**
8. **Future elite treatment with restrained Blue-origin identity details**

The sequence represents:

- base identity
- within-level advancement
- formal material promotion
- advancement toward next material
- next formal promotion
- elite gemstone progression

---

## 16. Generalized Prestige Ladder

The reusable pattern is:

### Current-level entry state

**Current material dominant + prior lineage accents**

### Current-level advancement state

**Current material dominant + next material accents**

### Next-level promotion state

**Next material dominant + prior material accents**

Then repeat.

**Example:**

- Blue
- Blue with Gold accents
- Gold with Blue accents
- Gold with Platinum accents
- Platinum with Gold accents
- Platinum with Diamond accents
- Platinum-Diamond with prior-lineage details

Prestige unlocks and theme-choice opportunities are historical achievement records.

The implementation must eventually distinguish:

- current active rank
- current visual state
- highest confirmed rank ever reached
- highest confirmed prestige milestone

## 20. Competitive Integrity

Identity themes must never affect gameplay fairness.

Themes must **not**:

- alter legal move validation
- alter rating math
- alter clocks
- create live analysis cues
- expose hidden information
- change board state
- change tournament authority
- create competitive visual advantage
- interfere with board readability

Trainer styling must remain isolated from live competitive assistance.

ACCL constitutional boundaries remain active:

- engine truth supremacy
- legal validation first
- AI subordinate to governance
- no live-board assistance
- Trainer post-game and training only
- tournament integrity
- audit reconstructability
- authority isolation

## 23. Summary Doctrine

ACCL player identity begins with one locked base color: **Red, Blue, Black, or White**.

The player earns the right to evolve that identity by surpassing prior confirmed peak achievements.

Optional style packages control the visual world, such as cute K-12 mascot themes, gothic courts, or angelic halls.

Earned prestige materials evolve through an intra-level visual ladder:

**current dominant color or material → next material appears as accents → next material becomes dominant after formal promotion**

**Example:**

**Blue → Blue with Gold accents → Gold with Blue accents → Gold with Platinum accents → Platinum with Gold accents**

Profile and domain surfaces may use dramatic cinematic presentations.

Trainer uses a cleaner version: solid dominant color or material with restrained supporting accents.

Players may buy stylistic worlds.

Players may **never** buy competitive rank, fake prestige, or advantage.

# ACCL Paid Cosmetic Add-On, Theme Package, and Progression Carry-Over Doctrine

ACCL may offer optional paid cosmetic add-ons that allow players to personalize their avatar transformation, profile domain, Trainer appearance, banners, motifs, and decorative atmosphere.

Paid add-ons are visual identity products.

They are not competitive products.

A player may purchase a world, style, color palette, seasonal presentation, or curated visual bundle.

A player may never purchase rating, rank, prestige status, tournament standing, gameplay advantage, or the appearance of an earned prestige material that they have not actually earned.

**Core rule:** Players may buy cosmetic worlds. Players may not buy competitive proof.

## 4. Permanent Ownership Rule

A paid add-on should remain owned permanently after purchase unless a specific limited-license product is clearly disclosed before purchase.

**Default rule:** Purchased cosmetic packages remain permanently owned.

A player may:

- select a purchased package
- unselect it
- return to Standard ACCL presentation
- reactivate the purchased package later
- carry it forward into higher progression blocks
- see it render at a lower visual block after downgrade
- retain ownership after downgrade
- retain ownership while inactive

Ownership and active presentation are separate concepts.

It should not remain frozen at the visual state in which it was purchased.

## 7. Upgrade Rendering Rule

When a player advances within or beyond a progression block, the active package should visually upgrade to the eligible package-aware variant.

General pattern:

```text
current dominant material
→ next material appears as accents
→ next material becomes dominant after formal promotion
```

Example for a Blue-origin player:

```text
Blue
→ Blue with Gold accents
→ Gold with Blue accents
→ Gold with Platinum accents
→ Platinum with Gold accents
```

Inside a purchased package:

```text
Blue Gothic Court
→ Blue Gothic Court with Gold accents
→ Gold Gothic Court with Blue accents
→ Gold Gothic Court with Platinum accents
→ Platinum Gothic Court with Gold accents
```

A package should support visual growth rather than functioning as a static wallpaper.

---

## 8. Downgrade Rendering Rule

When a player’s current competitive block downgrades, the active package should visually downgrade with it.

Example:

```text
Gold Gothic Court with Platinum accents
↓
Gold Gothic Court with Blue accents
↓
Silver Gothic Court with Gold accents
```

The player still owns Gothic Court.

The player simply no longer displays a higher current-block treatment that they are not presently entitled to show.

This preserves competitive meaning.

### Current active rendering

A player may preserve proof of past achievement in Vault or history.

The active competitive presentation should communicate present standing.

## 10. Carry-Forward Rule

A paid package should support carry-over into future levels.

A player who buys Gothic Court at an early level may want to see:

- Gold Gothic Court
- Platinum Gothic Court
- Lava Gothic Court
- Mercury Gothic Court

The package should not become obsolete when the player climbs.

Possible setting:

```text
Carry Active Package Forward
- On
- Off
```

**Carry-forward on:** The package remains active as the player advances or downgrades. The renderer automatically selects the eligible package-aware version for the player’s current block.

**Carry-forward off:** The package remains owned but may revert to Standard ACCL presentation after a major progression transition until the player manually reapplies it.

Default behavior remains open for product lock.

**Recommended default:** Carry Active Package Forward: On — best respects the player’s paid visual preference.

## 17. Seasonal Activation Options

A seasonal package may support one of several models.

**Possible models:**

- **Permanent ownership** — purchase once; reactivate in future seasons
- **Seasonal storefront availability** — purchase only during the season; owned permanently afterward
- **Temporary active window** — usable only during a disclosed seasonal window
- **Annual recurring access** — reactivates automatically each year during its designated season

Exact monetization rules remain open.

**Recommended baseline:** Seasonal storefront availability with permanent ownership after purchase — preserves player trust and increases long-term collection value.

## 26. Summary Doctrine (Paid Add-Ons)

ACCL paid add-ons are permanently owned cosmetic worlds.

Players may select or unselect owned packages.

An active package should render the player’s current progression block instead of remaining frozen at the visual state in which it was purchased.

Purchased packages may carry forward into future levels and should visually upgrade or downgrade with the player’s current competitive state.

The package defines the world.

The optional palette defines personality.

The earned progression material defines proof.

Players may buy style.

Players may buy atmosphere.

Players may buy color options.

Players may buy seasonal worlds.

Players may **never** buy rank.
